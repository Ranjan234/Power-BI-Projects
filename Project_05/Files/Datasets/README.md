# 📊 Customer Sales Dashboard – Power BI

## 📌 Project Overview

The **Customer Sales Dashboard** is an interactive Power BI project designed to analyze sales performance, customer behavior, product categories, seasonal trends, and customer ratings.

The dashboard helps users understand key business metrics and identify opportunities to improve sales performance and customer satisfaction through data-driven insights.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyze overall revenue and order performance.
- Identify the highest-revenue product categories.
- Understand sales contribution by gender.
- Compare revenue and order trends across seasons.
- Analyze discount usage and customer ratings.
- Present key business insights through an interactive dashboard.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI** – Dashboard development and visualization
- **Power Query** – Data cleaning and transformation
- **DAX** – Measures and KPI calculations
- **Data Visualization** – Charts, KPI cards, and interactive slicers

## 🔍 Key Business Insights

### 1. Product Category Performance
- Clothing is the leading category, generating approximately **$104.3K** in revenue.
- It contributes around **45% of total revenue**.
- Outerwear generated the lowest revenue, at approximately **$18.5K**.

### 2. Customer Gender Analysis
- Male customers contributed approximately **67.74%** of total revenue.
- Female customers contributed approximately **32.26%**.
- These results show differences in revenue contribution across customer groups.

### 3. Seasonal Sales Analysis
- **Fall was the strongest season for revenue**, followed by Winter.
- Summer recorded the lowest revenue among the four seasons.
- Businesses could investigate seasonal customer preferences and plan promotions accordingly.

### 4. Discount Analysis
- Approximately **43% of sales involved discounts**.
- Further analysis could help determine whether discounts increase order volume and revenue or reduce profit margins.

### 5. Customer Ratings
- The overall average customer rating was **3.75**.
- Footwear and Accessories recorded the highest category ratings, at approximately **3.8**.
- Reviewing customer feedback could help identify opportunities to improve satisfaction.

## 📊 Dashboard Features

- Interactive slicers for Gender, Subscription Status, Location, and Product
- KPI cards for revenue, orders, average rating, and discount usage
- Revenue analysis by product category
- Revenue contribution by gender
- Seasonal revenue and order trend analysis
- Average customer rating by category
- Interactive filtering for exploring different customer segments

## 💡 Business Recommendations

- Focus on Clothing, the highest-revenue category, while exploring opportunities to grow other categories.
- Investigate why Summer sales are lower and consider targeted seasonal campaigns.
- Evaluate discount effectiveness by comparing revenue, order volume, and profit margins.
- Analyze customer feedback to improve ratings and customer satisfaction.
- Use customer segmentation to develop more targeted marketing strategies.
