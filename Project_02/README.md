
# 📊 Amazon Sales Analysis Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard built to analyze Amazon product sales performance, tracking revenue, product movement, and customer reviews across categories.

## 📌 Problem Statement

The business needed a centralized dashboard to monitor sales performance and customer feedback throughout the year, enabling data-driven decisions on top-performing products and categories.

### KPI Requirements
- **YTD Sales** – Monitor year-to-date sales to gauge overall revenue performance over time.
- **QTD Sales** – Track quarterly sales figures to identify trends and fluctuations.
- **YTD Products Sold** – Analyze total products sold to understand product movement.
- **YTD Reviews** – Track year-to-date reviews to assess customer feedback and satisfaction.

### Chart Requirements
- **YTD Sales by Month (Line Chart)** – Visualize monthly sales trends to spot seasonal patterns.
- **YTD Sales by Week (Column Chart)** – Track weekly sales for short-term performance insights.
- **Sales by Product Category (Heat Map / Table)** – High-level overview of sales across categories.
- **Top 5 Products by YTD Sales (Bar Chart)** – Highlight top revenue-generating products.
- **Top 5 Products by YTD Reviews (Bar Chart)** – Identify top-rated products by customer preference.

## 🛠️ Tools Used
- **Power BI** – Data modeling, DAX measures, and dashboard design
- **Excel / CSV** – Source data cleaning and preparation

## 📈 Key Insights
- Total YTD Sales reached **$2.18M**, with QTD Sales at **$811.09K**.
- **27.75K** products sold and **19.42M** reviews tracked YTD.
- **Men Shoes** led all categories with **$940.27K** in YTD sales (43.18% of total).
- Top-selling product: **Nikon Wide Angle AF Nikkor 24mm** (~$34K in YTD sales).
- Top-reviewed product: **SanDisk 16GB 3-Pack Ultra microSD** (0.40M reviews).
